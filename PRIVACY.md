# Cadence — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 August 2026**

## The short version

Cadence keeps everything on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, and no
analytics. Your health data is never uploaded, sold, or shared — because Cadence
has no way to send it anywhere.

## What Cadence stores, and where

Everything Cadence records lives in a private database on your own device
(Apple's on-device storage for the app). Nothing is transmitted off the phone.
That includes:

- **Your profile:** first name, event, goal time, training days, experience
  level, plan start date, and an optional meet date.
- **Daily check-ins:** your answers to the five morning questions, plus optional
  tap-test and jump-test results.
- **Readiness results:** the score, verdict, and per-signal breakdown Cadence
  calculates each day.
- **Training log:** sessions, sets, reps, times, and personal records.

## Apple Health data

With your permission, Cadence **reads** the following from Apple Health to work
out how recovered you are:

- Heart rate variability (HRV)
- Resting heart rate
- Sleep analysis
- Respiratory rate
- Wrist temperature
- Workouts

Cadence reads this data only. It **never writes** anything to Apple Health, and
it never copies your Health data off the device. You grant this access in
Apple's own permission screen, and you can change or revoke it at any time in
the Health app under Sharing → Apps. Cadence works without it — the morning
check-in and phone tests alone can produce a readiness score.

## What Cadence does not do

- No user accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
- No servers or cloud backup operated by us.
- No analytics, crash reporting, telemetry, advertising, or tracking.
- No third-party SDKs. Cadence has no networking code at all.
- No selling or sharing of any data with anyone.

## Notifications

If you turn on notifications, they are scheduled and delivered **locally by your
iPhone**. Nothing is sent to a server to make them happen.

## Sharing you choose to do

If you tap Share on a PR card or a weekly recap, iOS creates an image and hands
it to whatever app you pick. That is the only time information leaves Cadence,
it contains only what you can see on the card (your first name, a time or your
week's totals, and a date), and it happens only when you choose it.

## Your control over your data

- **Delete everything:** Settings → Data → Reset all data. This permanently
  erases your profile, check-ins, readiness history, sessions, and records.
- **Delete the app:** removing Cadence deletes its database with it.
- **Export:** Settings → Data → Export history as CSV gives you your own copy.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our
side and no request you need to send us.

## Young athletes

Cadence is built for high-school-age sprinters. It collects no data centrally,
requires no account, and asks for no contact details, so there is nothing for us
to collect from a child. If you are under 13, please set Cadence up with a
parent or guardian. Parents: because all data stays on the device, you control it
by controlling the device — and you can erase everything from Settings at any
time.

## Not medical advice

Cadence estimates training readiness to help you decide how hard to train. It is
not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice.
Persistent low readiness, unusual symptoms, or pain warrant seeing a qualified
professional. Always train within your limits.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Since
Cadence has no way to contact you, please check back here.
